imagine yourself
in a room
filled with mirrors
what do you see?

the man you’ve become?
pitfalls succumbed?
hills overcome?
with resilience

what is your heart made of?
has love unraveled?
for passion, for friends, for family
for the hope of one day

what lies in your head?
thoughts of what it was
what it is, no past
no future, only now
