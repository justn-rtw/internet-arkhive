wish not
for the things you don’t have.
instead
be grateful for the things you do have.

everyone has a different purpose in life.
what it was
is the precursor
to what it is.

he who fears death
will never do anything
worthy of a man
who is alive.

what has been possible
has been done.
what is impossible
will be done.

practice like you never
got the chance to play
and play like
you’ve never won.

let the same hunger you had
when you had nothing
carry you through everything.
