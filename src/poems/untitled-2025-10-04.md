perusing the land of life,
riding down lovers lane,
detour to heartbreak hotel.
long stay, dark room.

heart wounds—
out the window,
moon looms.
darkness entombed.

moon glooms
yet there is light
paradoxical night.
checkout.

pain is beauty,
beauty in strife
beauty in life.
