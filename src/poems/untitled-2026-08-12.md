i’ve lived through
a million moments
where i thought
i wouldnt see the light of tomorrow.

my experiences are
the trophies i carry with me-
the burdens of a past me i no longer know
for he is reformed.

chiseled by the sculptor in the mirror-
gain and rain make common company,
for the sun keeps shining
and so will i.
